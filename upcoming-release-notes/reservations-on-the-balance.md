Show how a category balance divides up, in the place where you already read it.
Hold the pointer over the balance on desktop, or tap it on mobile. The breakdown
shows what is reserved for future costs, this month's allowance, and what is
spare. The goal panel gains a Committed line.

Mobile now takes reservations out of the balance in the same way as desktop, so
the two agree about the same category. The Committed column no longer appears in
the budget table on either one.

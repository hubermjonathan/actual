Show how a category's balance divides up where you already look for it: hover
the balance on desktop, tap it on mobile. The breakdown lists what is reserved
for future costs, this month's allowance and what is spare, and the goal panel
gains a Committed line. Mobile now takes reservations out of the balance as the
desktop does, so the two no longer disagree about the same category. The
Committed column is gone from the budget table on both.
